Account not signed in

Sign in and this device gets your token, key and settings back. They are locked with your password in this browser before they are sent, so what the server keeps is unreadable to anyone who reads its database — including you, without the password. Forget it and there is no way back in; nothing else can open the box. The password itself never leaves this page either: signing in sends something derived from it, and your server has no way to work backwards from that.

These two are what the account carries. Fill them in once, here, and any device you sign in on gets them.

Settings

Both keys are kept in this browser. The GitHub token is sent to api.github.com and the Anthropic key to api.anthropic.com, each to nowhere else. Neither reaches this page's source, the repository, or anyone else. Clear either by emptying its box and adding a card, or by clearing this site's data.

With an account they are also kept on your server, locked with your password before they leave this browser, so a new device is an email and a password rather than finding both keys again.

A key held in a browser is only as private as the device holding it, so it is worth giving the Anthropic key a spend limit in the Console. Leave the box empty and lookups go through a workflow instead: slower, but nothing is stored here.
